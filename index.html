    How to play

    A hidden circuit of logic gates drives the output Q. You can see its ; find gates that produce it in 6 .

    1. Tap a gate slot, then pick a gate. Keys 1–6 pick gates, arrow keys move between slots.
    2. Tap the inputs (or press A–D) to try values and watch them flow through your circuit; the truth table highlights that column and Q shows the wanted output.
    3. The formula under the circuit spells it out in (· AND, + OR, ⊕ XOR, ¬ NOT). Tap part of it to select that gate.
    4. Press Run circuit (Enter) to test it.
    5. Each slot gets a color, and the truth table shows which rows your circuit got right.
    6. Stuck? Open the helper to see the target as a Karnaugh map, with its simplest groupings.
    AND Right gate, right slot (✓).
    XOR Used in another slot (dashed ring).
    NOR Not used anywhere (or no more copies).

    Colors compare against one hidden circuit, but any wins. A new daily puzzle arrives at midnight and gets harder through the week.
