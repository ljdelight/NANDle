    How to play

    A hidden circuit of logic gates drives the output Q. You can see its truth table; find gates that produce it in 6 runs.

    1. Tap a gate slot, then pick a gate. Keys 1–6 pick gates, arrow keys move between slots.
    2. Tap the inputs (or press A–D) to try values and watch them flow through your circuit; the truth table highlights that column and Q shows the wanted output.
    3. The formula under the circuit spells it out in Boolean algebra (· AND, + OR, ⊕ XOR, ¬ NOT). Tap part of it to select that gate.
    4. Press Run circuit (Enter) to test it.
    5. Each slot gets a colour, and the truth table shows which rows your circuit got right.
    6. Stuck? Open the K-map helper to see the target as a Karnaugh map, with its simplest groupings.
    AND Right gate, right slot (✓).
    XOR Used in another slot (dashed ring).
    NOR Not used anywhere (or no more copies).

    Colours compare against one hidden circuit, but any circuit that matches the whole truth table wins. A new daily puzzle arrives at midnight and gets harder through the week.
